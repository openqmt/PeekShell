# Sponsor benefits

Thank you for supporting PeekShell. Sponsorship helps keep the project maintained and evolving — and it can unlock additional product capabilities for you.

## Unlockable features

Depending on your sponsorship, you may unlock more access, for example:

- **Terminal AI**: Use the AI assistant in terminal workflows for command suggestions and troubleshooting help
- **Custom backgrounds**: Personalize the app and terminal background for a more comfortable workspace
- **Cloud sync**: Sync hosts and settings across devices to avoid repeating setup

More benefits may be added over time based on community feedback.

## How to unlock

After sponsoring, contact maintainers via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/openqmt/PeekShell/issues) with your sponsorship details and the features you would like to unlock. We will help complete the activation.

Thank you again — your support keeps PeekShell moving forward.
